# Roadmap

## Done (first release)

- Seeded lycaa and Novin (historical, 2026-10-17) with provenance.
- Dashboard: CP, Item Level, DPS, CP gap, best next upgrade, data to collect, recommendations, progression, equipment, offensive stats, missing systems, unknowns, recent upgrades.
- Character: profile, combat stats catalog, core stats with what-if, Divine Stats with priority, Daevanion boards and nodes with route, skills and Stigmas with rotation notes, Arcana, titles, pet, wings, collections, freshness and conflict history.
- Equipment grid with item details (rolls, Soul Binding, Manastones) and an item-versus-item comparison.
- Compare: overview, advantages, disadvantages, missing systems, DPS- and CP-relevant differences, unknowns, catch-up plan, any two snapshots.
- Upgrade planner: ranking modes, best next upgrade with reasons, tasks with dependencies, materials, resources, targets.
- Pantheon: summary, display effects, collectibles with filters, ownership vs display, farming, comparison, targets.
- Progression charts and snapshot diffs; DPS log per boss.
- Research store and editable stat tiers per mode.
- Import: screenshots (AI or manual), pasted text, JSON, CSV; review, conflicts, duplicates.
- AI assistant with read-only tools and approval for changes.
- Export: JSON, snapshots, plan, CSV; backup and restore.
- Tests: 25 unit tests, 12 end-to-end checks.
