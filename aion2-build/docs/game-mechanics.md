# Game mechanics

Status of everything the Build Architect knows about AION 2 mechanics. Nothing here is a verified damage formula.

## Verified (tooltip mappings, source: AionFlex tooltips)

Core Stats: Might (Attack Increase), Dexterity (Evasion, Block, Critical Hit Resist), Intelligence (Status Effect Chance), Constitution (HP Increase), Precision (Accuracy Increase, Critical Hit Increase), Willpower (Status Effect Resist).

Divine Stats: Justice (Defense Increase, Perfect Chance), Freedom (Accuracy Increase, Evasion Increase), Illusion (Cooldown Reduction, Endurance Penetration), Life (HP Increase, Regeneration), Time (Combat Speed, Double Resist), Destruction (Attack Increase, Perfect Resist), Death (Critical Hit Increase, Regeneration Penetration), Wisdom (MP Cost Reduction, Double Chance), Destiny (MP Increase, Endurance), Space (Movement Speed, Block Increase).

The size of each effect per level is not recorded.

## Provisional (discussion-based, editable in the app)

Assassin boss PvE stat tiers:
- S+: Combat Speed, Critical Hit, Attack Increase %, Smite
- S: Back Attack Damage Boost, PvE Damage Boost, Double Chance, Weapon Damage Boost, Damage Boost
- A+: Back Attack, Attack, Max Attack
- A: Critical Damage Boost, Boss Damage Boost, Attack Bonus, Cooldown Reduction, Accuracy, Critical Attack
- B: Perfect Chance, PvE Attack, Boss Attack
- C (for DPS): Evasion, Block, Critical Hit Resist, Status Effect Resist, MP, other defensive stats

Divine Stat collection priority: Wisdom, Destruction, Illusion, Time, Death, Freedom, Justice, Space, Life, Destiny.

Dungeon AoE and Solo PvE rankings start as copies of the boss ranking; PvP is a future extension.

## Rules the app follows

- Distinct stats stay distinct (flat vs %, Back Attack vs Back Attack Damage Boost, Critical Hit rating vs displayed chance).
- Higher Combat Power is not treated as higher DPS. DPS comparisons use logged runs on the same boss.
- Impact is shown as High / Medium / Low / Unknown, never as an invented percentage.
